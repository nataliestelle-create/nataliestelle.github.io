Leadership

Natalie Slessor is one of Australia’s few social psychologists specialising in the built environment. For 30 years she has worked at the intersection of people, place and performance — as an occupier, consultant, developer and property management leader — always asking the same question: what does this place make possible?

Most recently she served as Executive Managing Director, APAC at CBRE, leading a property management business spanning 10 countries, 9,000 people and 4,500 assets. Under her leadership, the Pacific business thrived, growing revenue by 71% and more than doubling EBIT margin.

Before CBRE, she spent 11 years at Lendlease across workplace, development, and investment management — including a central role in winning a US$22bn Silicon Valley master-planned project for Google. Earlier she led workplace strategy at Macquarie Group, Woods Bagot, and the ground-breaking and seminal London place consultancy DEGW.

She is based in Sydney.
